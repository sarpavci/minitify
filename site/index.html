minitify · design

The reference material behind minitify, a small Spotify remote for the terminal: audio plays on your Mac with no dock icon and no Spotify app. These four pages are the authority for anything visual — the written summaries live in docs/.

  • 30 SECONDS · AUTOPLAYS The film One continuous take: the terminal starts it, the panel arrives, it grows through its three modes, the library opens, the panel is dragged to the edge and settles. 900 frames, scrubbable a frame at a time.
  • ELEVEN FEATURES · LIVE The motion system Five curves, four duration bands, and for every feature a beat map, the Core Animation or SF Symbols call that carries it, and its Reduce Motion fallback. Every demo is replayable at true size.
  • MINI · NORMAL · EXTENDED · LIBRARY The interface All three panel modes and the library window at actual size, draggable, in light and dark, over a real desktop.
  • PARAMETRIC The mark Five bars that play a level meter, fall into a play triangle and rise into an M. Every number behind it is on a slider.
Album artwork on these pages is a placeholder. minitify ships no artwork — every cover comes from Spotify at runtime.
